Welcome on bkup! What to do next?
1- get bkup locally to use the CLI
2- configure the bkup-config Secret
3- create a backup-map ConfigMap from the CLI
  > preview:  bkup -c KUBE_CONTEXT -n NAMESPACE -bm -p
  > apply:    bkup -c KUBE_CONTEXT -n NAMESPACE -bm -a
4- tweak the backup-map (replace the CHANGEME, configure the backup-types, remove unwanted namespaces, etc...)
5- Run a one-shot backup from CLI to validate your backup-map
  > bkup -c KUBE_CONTEXT -n NAMESPACE -j
6- Once validated, just wait for the cronjob to run.
... Your backups are now working!
